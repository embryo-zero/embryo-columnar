# Embryo Columnar

Embryo Columnar is Embryo-Zero's fork of [arrow](https://github.com/apache/arrow) (5580366, commit 5580366). It does fast columnar data format.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE.txt, NOTICE.txt) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
